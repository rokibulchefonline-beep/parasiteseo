Choosing an online ordering system is a big decision. Here are the questions to ask any provider before you sign.

## Costs

1. **Is there commission on each order?** If so, what percentage, and are there extra fees for promotion?
2. **What's the fixed monthly cost**, and what does it include?
3. **What are the card processing fees?**
4. **What's the contract length**, and are there exit fees?

## Features

5. **Is it branded to your restaurant**, on your own domain?
6. **Is there a mobile app** for iOS and Android?
7. **Does it integrate with your EPoS** and kitchen printers?
8. **Can you manage offers, loyalty and time slots?**
9. **Can you update your menu and allergen information yourself**, instantly?

## Data and support

10. **Do you own your customer data**, and can you export it?
11. **Is support UK-based and available during service hours?**
12. **What happens if the system goes down** on a Friday night?

## Ask for references

Talk to restaurants already using the system, ideally ones with a similar cuisine and order volume.
