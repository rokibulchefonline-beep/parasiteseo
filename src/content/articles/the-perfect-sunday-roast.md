The Sunday roast is less a recipe than a juggling act. Get the timings right and everything else follows.

## Work backwards

Decide when you want to eat and work backwards. Write the timings on a piece of paper and stick it to the fridge. Remember that meat needs to rest, which frees the oven for the potatoes and puddings.

## Crispy roast potatoes

- Use a floury variety such as Maris Piper or King Edward.
- Parboil for about 8–10 minutes, then drain and leave to steam dry.
- Shake them in the pan to rough up the edges.
- Roast in very hot fat in a preheated tin, turning once.

## Proper gravy

Use the roasting tin. Pour off most of the fat, stir a spoonful of flour into the juices, then whisk in hot stock a little at a time. Simmer, season and strain.

## Yorkshire puddings

Equal volumes of eggs, plain flour and milk, rested batter and smoking-hot fat. Don't open the oven door while they rise.

| Element | Tip |
| --- | --- |
| Beef | Rest for at least 20 minutes |
| Potatoes | Hot fat, hot oven |
| Yorkshires | Don't peek |
