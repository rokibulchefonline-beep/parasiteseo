You don't need to be a technology expert to protect yourself online. A few habits go a long way.

## Use a password manager

It creates and remembers a strong, different password for every account. Then you only need to remember one.

## Turn on two-step verification

Most email, banking and social media accounts offer it. It stops most attacks even if a password is stolen.

## Be sceptical of urgency

Scam messages often claim that something terrible will happen unless you act immediately. Slow down, and contact the organisation using a number you already know.

## Keep devices up to date

Updates fix security holes. Turn on automatic updates wherever you can.

## Report it

In the UK you can forward suspicious texts to **7726** and suspicious emails to **report@phishing.gov.uk**. If you've lost money, contact your bank straight away.
