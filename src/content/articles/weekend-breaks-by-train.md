Travelling by train turns the journey into part of the holiday. Here are five kinds of weekend that work particularly well without a car.

## 1. A cathedral city

Compact historic centres such as those in York, Durham or Canterbury are easy to explore on foot from the station.

## 2. The seaside

Many coastal towns grew up because of the railway, so stations are often a short walk from the front.

## 3. A scenic line

Some routes are destinations in themselves. Pack a flask, get a window seat and enjoy the view.

## 4. A national park gateway

Several national parks can be reached by train, with local buses or walking routes from the station.

## 5. A city you've never visited

Pick somewhere on your line you've only ever travelled through. You may be surprised.

## Saving money

Booking in advance, travelling off-peak and using a railcard can bring the cost down considerably. Check the National Rail website for the options that suit you.
