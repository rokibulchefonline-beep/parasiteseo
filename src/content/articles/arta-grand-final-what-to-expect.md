After months of public voting and assessment, the **Asian Restaurant & Takeaway Awards (ARTA)** reach their climax at the **Grand Final**, one of the biggest nights in the British Asian hospitality calendar.

Here's what happens, who attends, and why it matters to the restaurants and takeaways taking part.

## When is the Grand Final?

The **2026 ARTA Grand Final** takes place in **October 2026**. Check the [ARTA website](https://www.artauk.com/awards) for the confirmed date, venue and details.

## The road to the final

The Grand Final is the last stage of a process that starts at the beginning of the year.

### Public voting

Voting opens in **January**. For the 2026 awards, voting opened on **1 January 2026**. Diners across the UK nominate and vote for their favourite Asian restaurants and takeaways, rating them on **quality of food**, **quality of service** and **value for money**.

### Assessment

Venues are then assessed on:

- their **Customer Nomination Score**, from public votes
- their **ARTA Score**, based on **Tripadvisor reviews**, **Google reviews** and **food hygiene ratings**.

### Finalists

The highest-ranked venues progress through the awards process, and **finalists** from across the UK's regions are invited to the Grand Final.

## Who attends

The Grand Final brings together a wide cross-section of the industry:

- **finalist restaurants and takeaways**: owners, chefs and staff, often with their families
- **sponsors and partners** from across the food, drink and hospitality sectors
- **industry figures** and community leaders
- **guests** and supporters.

For many finalists, it's a rare chance to step away from the kitchen, dress up and celebrate with others who understand the demands of the business.

## The awards

### Regional winners

The UK's regions each have their own winners, recognising the best restaurants and takeaways in each part of the country. For local restaurants, a regional win is a significant achievement, and a powerful message to customers in their area.

### Champion of Champions

The evening culminates in the crowning of the **ARTA Champion of Champions**: one exceptional establishment chosen from among the finalists and presented with the **ARTA trophy**. It's the highest honour of the awards.

## The atmosphere

Awards nights in British Asian hospitality are celebrations: of food, of family businesses, of communities, and of an industry that has become part of British life. Expect:

- a **gala dinner**
- **entertainment**
- **speeches** recognising the industry's contribution and challenges
- **networking** among owners, chefs and suppliers
- plenty of **photographs** and **celebration**.

## Why it matters to finalists

### Recognition

Being named a finalist, let alone a winner, recognises years of hard work. For many family-run businesses, it's a moment of real pride.

### Publicity

Finalists and winners can share the news with customers, local media and on social media, which can bring in new customers and strengthen loyalty among existing ones.

### Team morale

Taking staff to the Grand Final, or celebrating with them afterwards, shows appreciation for their contribution.

### Connections

The event is an opportunity to meet peers, share experiences and build relationships with suppliers and partners.

## Tips for finalists

If your restaurant is a finalist:

### Before the event

- **Confirm attendance** and guest numbers promptly.
- **Arrange cover** for the restaurant on the night, or plan reduced hours.
- **Share the news** that you're a finalist with customers and local media.
- Prepare **photos**, your **restaurant's story** and a short **description** in case media or organisers ask.

### On the night

- **Enjoy it.** You've earned it.
- Take **photos** of your team.
- **Network** with other owners, suppliers and sponsors.

### Afterwards

- **Thank customers** for their support, whatever the result.
- **Share photos** and news on social media.
- Update your **website**, **Google Business Profile** and **ordering pages** with your finalist or winner status.
- Send a **press release** to local media.
- Display **certificates** or **trophies** in the restaurant.

## Sponsors and partners

The awards are supported by sponsors from across the hospitality and food industry. For businesses serving Asian restaurants, such as food and drink suppliers, equipment makers, technology providers and financial services, sponsorship offers visibility with owners and decision-makers. Details are on the [ARTA sponsors page](https://www.artauk.com/sponsors).

## Following the results

If you can't attend, you can follow along:

- **winners** are announced on the ARTA website and social media after the event
- **photos** and **highlights** are usually shared online.

Winners' lists are a great guide to highly rated restaurants and takeaways, so you might find a new favourite near you.

## The bottom line

The ARTA Grand Final is the culmination of months of public voting and assessment, and a celebration of British Asian hospitality. For finalists it's a night of recognition and pride, and for diners it's a guide to some of the best restaurants and takeaways in the country.
