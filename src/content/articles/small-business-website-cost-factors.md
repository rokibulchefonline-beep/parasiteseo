Ask five agencies to quote for a website and you may get five very different prices. Here's what drives the cost, and how to compare quotes.

## What affects the price

- **Number of pages and templates.** A five-page brochure site is very different from a 50-page site with a blog.
- **Custom design vs template.** Bespoke design takes more time than adapting a theme.
- **Functionality.** Online booking, e-commerce, customer accounts and integrations with other systems all add complexity.
- **Content.** Will you supply text and photos, or do you need copywriting and photography?
- **SEO setup.** Keyword research, technical SEO and migration from an old site.
- **Ongoing costs.** Hosting, domain, security updates, backups and support.

## Questions to ask every agency

1. What exactly is included, and what costs extra?
2. Who owns the website and the domain when it's finished?
3. What platform will it be built on, and can another developer take it over later?
4. How will it be optimised for speed, mobile and search engines?
5. What are the ongoing monthly or annual costs?
6. Can I see examples of similar sites you've built?

## Beware the cheapest option

A very low price may mean a poor template, no SEO, or hidden ongoing fees. A website is a sales tool, so judge it on the enquiries it brings in.
