Letting a property in England comes with a long list of legal responsibilities. Missing one can lead to fines, invalid notices or worse. Here is the core checklist.

## Safety

- **Gas safety**: an annual check by a Gas Safe registered engineer, with a copy given to tenants.
- **Electrical safety**: an inspection (EICR) at least every five years, with a copy given to tenants.
- **Smoke alarms** on every storey used as living accommodation.
- **Carbon monoxide alarms** in any room with a fixed combustion appliance (excluding gas cookers).
- **Fire safety** for furnishings, and additional duties for HMOs and blocks of flats.

## Energy efficiency

A valid **EPC** is required, and rented homes must generally meet a minimum rating of **E**, unless a valid exemption is registered.

## Deposits

Protect the deposit in a government-approved scheme **within 30 days** and give the tenant the prescribed information.

## Right to Rent

Check that all adult occupiers have the right to rent in England before the tenancy starts, and keep records.

## Documents to give tenants

The current "How to Rent" guide, gas safety certificate, EPC and EICR.

## Licensing

HMOs above a certain size need a mandatory licence, and many London boroughs, including in East London, run additional or selective licensing schemes. Check your council's website.

## The Renters' Rights Act

The Act, which became law in 2025, is reshaping private renting in England, including ending section 21 "no-fault" evictions and changing how tenancies and rent increases work. Keep up to date on GOV.UK, as requirements are being introduced in stages.
