Britain's love of curry is part of the national identity. The **Asian Restaurant & Takeaway Awards (ARTA)** exist to celebrate the restaurants, takeaways, chefs and hospitality teams behind it.

## What ARTA is

ARTA is a nationwide awards programme recognising outstanding British Asian restaurants and takeaways. First held in 2018, it has become known in the industry as the "Oscars of the Curry Industry".

## Who can take part

According to the organisers, every Asian restaurant and takeaway in the United Kingdom is eligible to be nominated. That includes Bangladeshi, Indian, Pakistani, Nepalese, Sri Lankan and other South Asian cuisines.

## How it works

1. **Public voting.** Diners across the UK vote for their favourite restaurant or takeaway. For 2026, voting opened on 1 January.
2. **Assessment.** The highest-ranked venues are assessed on customer votes and on independent measures such as online reviews and food hygiene ratings.
3. **Grand Final.** Finalists gather for the Grand Final, where regional winners are announced and one venue is named the **ARTA Champion of Champions**. The 2026 Grand Final takes place in October.

## Why it matters

For small, family-run restaurants, recognition from their own customers can be a real boost to morale and business. Awards also shine a light on the chefs and staff who keep Britain's curry houses going.
