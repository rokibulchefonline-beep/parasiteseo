Every neighbourhood has a curry house or takeaway its customers swear by. The one that remembers your order, where the chef always sends out something extra, and whose lamb bhuna you'd defend against all comers.

Public-vote awards give you a way to say thank you. Here's how nominations work for the **Asian Restaurant & Takeaway Awards (ARTA)**, and how to make your vote count.

## Why your vote matters

At [ARTA](https://www.artauk.com/), the first stage of the awards is driven by the public. Diners nominate and vote for their favourite Asian restaurants and takeaways, and the highest-ranked venues go forward in the competition.

For a small, independent restaurant, a nomination from its own customers is powerful recognition. Many owners and staff work long hours for years with little public thanks. Knowing their customers took the time to vote can mean a great deal.

## Who can you nominate?

According to ARTA, **every Asian restaurant and takeaway in the UK** is eligible. That includes:

- traditional **British curry houses**
- **Bangladeshi**, **Indian**, **Pakistani**, **Nepalese** and **Sri Lankan** restaurants
- **takeaways** and **delivery-only kitchens**
- **fine dining** restaurants and **family-run cafés**.

It doesn't matter whether the venue is big or small, famous or undiscovered.

## When can you vote?

Voting for each year's awards opens in **January**. For the 2026 awards, voting opened on **1 January 2026**. Check the ARTA website for the current year's dates and deadlines.

## What you'll be asked to rate

ARTA's **Customer Nomination Score** is based on three things. Before you vote, think about your recent visits or orders.

### Quality of food

- Is the food consistently delicious?
- Are the dishes well cooked, well spiced and fresh?
- Are portions generous and presentation good?
- Does it arrive hot, if it's a takeaway?

### Quality of service

- Are the staff friendly and welcoming?
- Are orders accurate?
- Is the restaurant clean and comfortable?
- Are problems handled well?
- For takeaways: is delivery on time, and is phone or online ordering easy?

### Value for money

- Do you feel the food is worth the price?
- Are portions and quality fair for what you pay?

Value for money doesn't mean cheapest. A more expensive restaurant can offer excellent value if the quality justifies the price.

## How to nominate: step by step

1. **Go to the [ARTA nomination page](https://www.artauk.com/nominate).**
2. **Find the restaurant or takeaway** you want to nominate.
3. **Complete the form**, rating the venue honestly on food, service and value.
4. **Submit your nomination**, following any instructions to confirm your vote.

Keep an eye on ARTA's website and social media for updates as the awards progress.

## Play fair

Awards only mean something if the votes are genuine. To keep the process fair:

- **Vote once**, following the rules for your nomination.
- **Rate honestly.** Your ratings should reflect your real experience.
- **Encourage other genuine customers** to vote if they've enjoyed the restaurant too.
- **Don't create fake entries** or use multiple accounts.
- **Don't offer or accept incentives** for votes.

ARTA's assessment also considers independent measures, including **online reviews** and **food hygiene ratings**, so a genuine reputation built over time counts for more than a last-minute voting drive.

## Tell the restaurant

After you've voted, let the staff know. Mention it when you next order or visit, or post on their social media. For a family-run business, it can make someone's week, and it may encourage other customers to vote too.

## Leave a review as well

While you're thinking about what makes your favourite restaurant special, consider leaving a **Google** or **Tripadvisor review**. Detailed, genuine reviews help other diners discover great restaurants. And since ARTA's assessment includes online reviews, they help the restaurant's standing too.

A helpful review mentions:

- specific dishes you enjoyed
- the service and atmosphere
- whether you ate in or ordered a takeaway
- what makes the restaurant stand out.

## Follow the journey

Once voting closes, the highest-ranked venues progress through the awards. Finalists are invited to the **ARTA Grand Final**, where regional winners are announced and one venue is crowned **Champion of Champions**. The 2026 Grand Final takes place in **October 2026**.

If your favourite restaurant makes the shortlist, you'll have played a part in its success.

## Discover new favourites

Award shortlists are also a great way to discover new places. Check the finalists and winners in your region, and next time you're choosing where to eat, try one you haven't been to before.

## The bottom line

Nominating your favourite curry house takes a few minutes and means a lot to the people behind it. Rate honestly on food, service and value, play fair, tell the restaurant, and leave a genuine review.
