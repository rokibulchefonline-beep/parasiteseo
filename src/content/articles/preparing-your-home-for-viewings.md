Buyers and tenants often decide how they feel about a home within minutes. These quick wins help yours make the right impression.

1. **Declutter.** Clear worktops, shelves and floors so rooms feel bigger.
2. **Deep clean.** Kitchens and bathrooms especially. Clean windows let in more light.
3. **Fix the small things.** Dripping taps, loose handles, blown bulbs.
4. **Neutralise.** A fresh coat of light, neutral paint helps people picture their own things in the space.
5. **Let the light in.** Open curtains and blinds and turn on lamps for evening viewings.
6. **Show each room's purpose.** A box room should look like a bedroom or office, not a store cupboard.
7. **Tidy the entrance.** Hallways and front doors set the tone. For flats, check that communal areas are presentable.
8. **Fresh air.** Air rooms before viewings and avoid strong cooking smells.
9. **Pets out of sight.** Not everyone is an animal lover.
10. **Have paperwork ready.** EPC, service charge details and utility information help serious buyers move quickly.

## Photos matter too

Most buyers and tenants see your home online first. Professional photography and a floor plan are worth it.
