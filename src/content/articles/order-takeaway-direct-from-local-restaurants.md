When you order a takeaway, the restaurant rarely keeps the whole of what you pay. Many large delivery marketplaces charge restaurants a commission on every order, and on a small independent's margins that can decide whether a busy Friday night makes money at all.

## How commission works

Marketplace apps usually take a percentage of each order in return for listing the restaurant and, in some cases, delivering the food. The restaurant still pays for ingredients, staff, rent and energy. When the commission goes up, restaurants often have to choose between raising menu prices and accepting thinner margins.

## What "ordering direct" means

Ordering direct means buying from the restaurant's own website or app, or through a platform that doesn't take a cut of each order. [ChefOnline](https://www.chefonline.co.uk/) is one example. It lists UK restaurants and takeaways and charges them no commission on orders, and customers pay no handling fee.

## Why it matters to you

- **Fairer prices.** Restaurants that aren't paying commission have less reason to inflate prices online.
- **Better communication.** If there's an issue with your order, you're dealing with the people who cooked it.
- **Local loyalty.** Some restaurants reward direct customers with offers and loyalty discounts.
- **Keeping independents open.** Every order that stays with the restaurant helps a local business survive.

## How to do it

1. Search for your favourite restaurant by name and look for "order online" on its own website.
2. Check whether it's listed on a commission-free platform.
3. If you usually order through a marketplace, try the direct route next time and compare the price.

It's a small change to your routine, and it can make a real difference to the places you love.
