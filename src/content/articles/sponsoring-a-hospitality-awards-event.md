If your business sells to restaurants, whether food supplies, drinks, equipment, technology or financial services, sponsoring an industry awards event can put you in front of exactly the right audience.

## What sponsors typically gain

- **Brand visibility** at the event, on the awards website and across social media.
- **Credibility** through association with recognising excellence.
- **Networking** with restaurant owners, chefs and decision-makers in one room.
- **Content**: photos, videos and stories to use in your own marketing.

## Questions to ask organisers

1. Who attends, and how many guests are expected?
2. What is included in each sponsorship package?
3. What reach do the awards have online and in the media?
4. Can you sponsor a specific category that fits your brand?
5. What reporting will you receive afterwards?

## Make it work harder

- Plan follow-up with the contacts you make.
- Offer something useful to attendees, such as a demonstration, a guide or a special offer.
- Share the event on your own channels before, during and after.
