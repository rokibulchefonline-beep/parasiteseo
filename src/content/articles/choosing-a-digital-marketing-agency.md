Hiring a digital marketing agency is an investment. These questions will help you choose well.

1. **What results have you achieved for businesses like mine?** Ask for case studies and references.
2. **Who will actually work on my account?** Meet the people, not just the salesperson.
3. **What will you do in the first 90 days?** A good agency will have a clear plan.
4. **How will you measure success?** Look for enquiries, sales and revenue, not just traffic or impressions.
5. **How often will you report, and what will reports include?**
6. **Do I own my accounts and data?** Your Google Ads, Analytics and website accounts should be in your name.
7. **What's the contract length and notice period?**
8. **What's included in the fee, and what costs extra?** Clarify ad spend vs management fees.
9. **What accreditations do you hold?** Google Partner or Meta partner status are helpful signals. Verify them in the platforms' own directories.
10. **What would you not recommend for my business?** Honest agencies will tell you what won't work.

## Red flags

- guaranteed number-one rankings on Google
- vague reporting
- long lock-in contracts with no performance review
- buying links or fake reviews.
