In many takeaways, online orders still arrive on a tablet and are re-typed into the till by hand. It works, until the Saturday rush.

## The problems with separate systems

- **Re-keying errors**: a missed side dish, the wrong spice level.
- **Slower service** at the busiest times.
- **Messy reporting**: sales split across different systems make it hard to see what's selling.
- **Menu mismatches**: a dish sells out in the restaurant but is still available online.

## What integration gives you

- online orders printed straight to the kitchen
- one menu updated in one place
- a single view of sales across dine-in, collection, delivery and online
- simpler end-of-day cash-up and VAT records.

## What to look for in an EPoS

- designed for hospitality, with table plans, courses, modifiers and kitchen printing
- works with your online ordering system
- reliable offline mode if the internet drops
- clear reporting on best sellers and busy times
- UK-based support available during evening service.

## Making Tax Digital

HMRC's Making Tax Digital rules are gradually extending digital record-keeping requirements. A good EPoS makes accurate digital records much easier. Check GOV.UK for how the current rules apply to your business.
