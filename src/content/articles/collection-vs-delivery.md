Delivery is one of the great conveniences of modern life. Tap a few buttons and dinner arrives at your door. But if your favourite restaurant is a short walk or drive away, collecting your order yourself can be the smarter choice for your wallet, your dinner and the restaurant.

## Why collection often costs less

### No delivery fee

Most delivery orders carry a delivery charge, and some also have a service fee or a small-order fee if you don't reach a minimum spend. Collection orders usually avoid all of these.

### Collection discounts

Many independent restaurants and takeaways offer a percentage off collection orders. It's not unusual to see 10% off collection, sometimes more on quieter nights. Restaurants can afford it because collection saves them the cost of a driver, fuel and time.

### Lower minimum orders

Delivery often comes with a minimum order value, which can push you into ordering more than you need. Collection rarely has the same restriction, so you can order exactly what you want.

### Tipping

With delivery, it's good practice to tip the driver. With collection, you can choose to tip the kitchen team instead, or not at all. It's entirely up to you.

## It's usually faster

With delivery, your food waits for a driver who may be finishing another drop or stuck in traffic. With collection, your food is ready when you arrive, as long as you time it right.

On a busy Friday or Saturday night, when delivery times can stretch well beyond an hour, collection can save a lot of waiting.

## It arrives hotter and fresher

The shorter the journey, the better the food. Some dishes suffer more than others on a long delivery:

- **fried starters**, such as onion bhajis, pakoras and samosas, lose their crunch
- **chips** go soft
- **naan and other breads** turn chewy
- **rice** can become clumpy
- **pizza** goes soggy under the lid.

Collect it yourself and eat it within 10 or 15 minutes, and the difference is noticeable.

## You can check your order before you leave

Collecting gives you the chance to check your bag at the counter. If something is missing, it can be put right on the spot, not after a phone call and a long wait.

## When delivery makes more sense

Collection isn't always practical. Delivery is the better option when:

- you don't have a car, or the restaurant isn't within walking distance
- the weather is awful
- you're hosting and can't leave the house
- you're unwell or have mobility needs
- you've had a drink and can't drive.

There's no point saving a few pounds if it means driving when you shouldn't, or dragging yourself out when you're ill.

## How to time your collection perfectly

1. **Order ahead.** Most online ordering systems let you choose a collection time slot.
2. **Work out your journey.** Pick a slot that matches how long it takes you to get there.
3. **Allow for busy nights.** Kitchens run slower during peak times, so add a few minutes.
4. **Check your confirmation.** It should show the estimated ready time.
5. **Arrive on time.** Food left on the counter for too long cools down too.

## Collection tips from restaurant staff

People who work in takeaways often have the same requests:

- **Give your name clearly** when you arrive, along with the order number if you have one.
- **Park considerately.** Many takeaways are on busy high streets, so don't block the road.
- **Bring a bag for large orders**, or a box to keep containers level in the car.
- **Keep dishes upright.** Curry containers don't like sharp corners.

## Making the most of collection offers

If you order regularly, ask whether the restaurant has a loyalty scheme for collection customers. Some offer a free side, a free starter or a discount after a certain number of orders.

## The environmental angle

If you're walking or cycling to collect your food, you're also cutting down on car journeys. Even driving yourself can be more efficient if you're passing the restaurant anyway on your way home.

## Collection vs delivery: a quick comparison

| | Collection | Delivery |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Delivery fee | None | Usually |
| Discounts | Often available | Less common |
| Speed | Ready when you arrive | Depends on drivers |
| Food temperature | Hotter | Can cool in transit |
| Convenience | You travel | Comes to you |
| Best for | Nearby restaurants, regulars | Bad weather, no transport, hosting |

## The bottom line

If the restaurant is nearby and you can get there easily, collection is usually cheaper, faster and tastier. Save delivery for the nights when staying in really matters, and enjoy the savings the rest of the time.
