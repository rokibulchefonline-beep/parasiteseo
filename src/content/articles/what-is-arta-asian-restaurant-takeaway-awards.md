Britain's love of curry is part of the national identity. From high street curry houses to neighbourhood takeaways, British Asian restaurants feed millions of people every week, employ tens of thousands, and have shaped the way the country eats.

The **Asian Restaurant & Takeaway Awards (ARTA)** exist to celebrate the people behind that success: the restaurants, takeaways, chefs and hospitality teams who keep Britain's curry culture thriving.

## What ARTA is

ARTA is a nationwide awards programme recognising outstanding British Asian restaurants and takeaways. First held in **2018**, it has become known in the industry as the **"Oscars of the Curry Industry"**.

The awards cover the full breadth of South Asian cuisine in the UK: Bangladeshi, Indian, Pakistani, Nepalese, Sri Lankan and more, in restaurants and takeaways of every size, from single family-run takeaways to well-known restaurants.

## Who can take part

According to the organisers, **every Asian restaurant and takeaway in the United Kingdom** is eligible to be nominated. There's no requirement to be a member of an organisation or to pay to enter. The process starts with the public.

## How it works

### 1. Public voting

Each year, members of the public are invited to **nominate and vote** for their favourite Asian restaurant or takeaway. For the 2026 awards, public voting opened on **1 January 2026**.

Voters rate their chosen venue on:

- **quality of food**
- **quality of service**
- **value for money**.

These ratings form the venue's **Customer Nomination Score**.

### 2. Independent assessment

Public votes aren't the only factor. ARTA also assesses venues on an **ARTA Score**, based on publicly available information:

- **Tripadvisor reviews**
- **Google reviews**
- **food hygiene ratings**.

Combining customer votes with independent measures means a restaurant can't succeed on popularity alone. It also needs a consistent reputation and a well-run kitchen.

### 3. Progressing through the awards

The highest-ranked venues progress through the awards process, with finalists chosen from across the UK's regions.

### 4. The Grand Final

Finalists gather at the **ARTA Grand Final**, where **regional winners** are announced and one exceptional establishment is crowned the **ARTA Champion of Champions** and presented with the ARTA trophy. The **2026 Grand Final** takes place in **October 2026**.

## Why ARTA matters

### Recognition for hard work

Running a restaurant or takeaway is demanding: long hours, late nights, rising costs and fierce competition. For small, family-run businesses in particular, public recognition from their own customers can mean a great deal to owners and staff.

### Celebrating a British success story

British Asian restaurants are one of the great stories of British hospitality. Many were founded by families who came to the UK from Bangladesh, Pakistan and India in the decades after the Second World War. They built businesses from nothing and created a cuisine that became part of British life. ARTA celebrates that legacy and the new generations continuing it.

### Raising standards

Because the assessment includes hygiene ratings and online reviews, the awards reward the fundamentals: great food, good service, fair prices and high standards of food safety. Those are things every diner cares about.

### Helping diners find great food

Shortlists and winners give diners a useful guide to highly rated restaurants and takeaways in their area, including hidden gems that might not spend much on advertising.

## Challenges facing the industry

The awards also shine a light on the challenges British Asian restaurants face today, including:

- **rising costs**: ingredients, energy, rent and wages
- **staff shortages**, particularly skilled chefs
- **changing habits**, with more people ordering takeaways and fewer dining out
- **commission** on delivery marketplace orders.

Recognition and publicity can help restaurants attract customers and staff in a tough market.

## Sponsors and partners

ARTA is supported by **sponsors** from across the food, drink and hospitality sectors. For businesses that serve Asian restaurants and takeaways, sponsorship offers a way to reach owners and decision-makers. Details are available on the [ARTA sponsors page](https://www.artauk.com/sponsors).

## How diners can get involved

- **Nominate** your favourite restaurant or takeaway when voting opens.
- **Rate it honestly** on food, service and value.
- **Leave genuine reviews** on Google and Tripadvisor.
- **Follow the results** and try the finalists near you.

## How restaurants can get involved

- Make sure your **food hygiene rating**, **Google** and **Tripadvisor** listings are accurate and up to date.
- Let your **regular customers** know when voting opens, without offering incentives for votes.
- Focus on the basics every day: great food, friendly service and fair value.

Visit the [ARTA website](https://www.artauk.com/about) for the latest news, nomination details, FAQs, finalists and winners.

## The bottom line

ARTA celebrates the British Asian restaurants and takeaways that have become part of the fabric of the UK. By combining public votes with independent measures of quality and hygiene, it recognises venues that deliver great experiences day in, day out.
