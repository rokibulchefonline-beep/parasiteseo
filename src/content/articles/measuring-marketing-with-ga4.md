If you can't measure your marketing, you can't tell what's working. Google Analytics 4 (GA4) is the free tool most businesses use to understand their website visitors.

## Focus on key events

GA4 tracks page views automatically, but the real value is in tracking the actions that matter:

- contact form submissions
- phone number clicks
- online bookings or orders
- newsletter sign-ups
- purchases.

Mark these as **key events** so you can see which channels drive results.

## Understand your traffic sources

GA4 shows where visitors come from: organic search, paid ads, social media, email or other websites. Tag links in your campaigns with UTM parameters so you can tell them apart.

## Link it up

Connect GA4 with Google Search Console and Google Ads to see search queries and ad performance alongside website behaviour.

## Respect privacy

In the UK, analytics cookies are generally not "strictly necessary", so under PECR you normally need visitors' consent before setting them. Use a proper consent banner, respect people who say no, and explain your use of analytics in your privacy notice. The ICO publishes guidance on cookies.

## Review regularly

Set aside time each month to look at what's changed, which pages bring enquiries, and where visitors drop off.
