Your website is often the first impression a hungry customer gets. A clunky site loses orders, and a fast, clear one wins them. Here's what matters.

## 1. Mobile first

Most takeaway orders are placed on a phone. Test your site on a small screen: can a customer find the menu, add dishes and pay with their thumb alone?

## 2. A menu that sells

- clear categories: starters, mains, sides, breads, drinks
- short, appetising descriptions
- good photos of your best sellers
- allergen information available before checkout (a legal requirement for distance selling).

## 3. Fast, simple checkout

Guest checkout, saved addresses for regulars, and Apple Pay or Google Pay all reduce drop-off.

## 4. Collection and delivery options

Show delivery areas, minimum orders and estimated times clearly. Offer collection time slots.

## 5. Found on Google

Your site should load quickly, have your name, address and phone number on every page, and link to your Google Business Profile.

## 6. Your brand, not someone else's

A website on your own domain builds your brand and keeps customers coming back to you rather than to a marketplace full of competitors.
