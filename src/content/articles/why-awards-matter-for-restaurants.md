For an independent restaurant, marketing budgets are tight and competition is fierce. Industry awards can offer a boost that's hard to buy.

## Credibility

An award, or even a shortlisting, is independent evidence of quality. It gives new customers a reason to try you.

## Local press and social media

Local newspapers, radio and community groups love a local success story. Being named a finalist or winner creates a natural moment for coverage.

## Team morale

Hospitality is hard work. Public recognition is a reward for chefs, waiters and delivery drivers who rarely get thanked in public.

## Customer engagement

Public-vote awards such as the [Asian Restaurant & Takeaway Awards (ARTA)](https://www.artauk.com/) give restaurants a reason to connect with their regulars and ask for their support.

## A benchmark for improvement

Award criteria, such as hygiene ratings, online reviews, service and value, are a useful checklist for any restaurant, whether or not it wins.

## Making the most of recognition

- display certificates and trophies where customers can see them
- add award badges to your website, menus and ordering pages
- share the news on social media and your Google Business Profile
- thank your customers publicly.
