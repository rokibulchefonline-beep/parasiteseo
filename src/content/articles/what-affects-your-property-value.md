Estate agents are often asked, "What will add value to my home?" The honest answer is that some things matter a lot and others much less than people expect.

## Things you can't change

- **Location**: transport links, schools, amenities and the street itself.
- **The market**: interest rates, mortgage availability and local demand.
- **Tenure**: freehold vs leasehold, and for leaseholds the **length of the lease**. Short leases (particularly under about 80 years) can significantly affect value and mortgageability.

## Things that usually add value

- **Extra space**: a loft conversion, extension or an additional bedroom.
- **Kitchens and bathrooms** in good, neutral condition.
- **Energy efficiency**: a better EPC rating is increasingly important to buyers and lenders.
- **Outside space**, especially in cities.
- **Parking**, where it's scarce.

## Things that help it sell, even if they don't add much value

- fresh neutral paint
- fixing small repairs
- decluttering
- good photos and a clear floor plan.

## Things that rarely pay back

Very personal décor, high-end finishes above the level of the local market, and swimming pools in most UK locations.
