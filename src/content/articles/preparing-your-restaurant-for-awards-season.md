Awards that combine public votes with independent measures reward restaurants that get the basics right every day. Here's how to prepare.

## 1. Food hygiene

Review your processes, training and records well before your next inspection. A food hygiene rating of 5 is one of the strongest signals you can send to customers and judges alike.

## 2. Online reviews

- Ask satisfied customers to leave reviews on Google and Tripadvisor.
- Respond to every review, courteously and constructively.
- Never post or buy fake reviews. It breaks platform rules and UK consumer law.

## 3. Service

Brief your team on greeting guests, handling complaints and getting orders right. Consistency matters more than occasional brilliance.

## 4. Value for money

Customers judge value against quality and portion size, not just price. Make sure your menu descriptions match what arrives on the plate.

## 5. Engage your regulars, honestly

When public voting opens, let customers know with a table card, a note in takeaway bags or a social media post. Ask them to vote if they've enjoyed their experience. Never offer incentives in exchange for votes.

## 6. Keep your information up to date

Make sure your address, opening hours and contact details are consistent across your website, Google Business Profile and ordering platforms.
