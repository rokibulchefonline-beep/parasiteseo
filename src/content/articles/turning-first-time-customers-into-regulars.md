Every takeaway knows the value of its regulars. Here's how to turn more first-time customers into regulars.

## Get the first order right

Nothing builds loyalty like food that arrives hot, on time and correct. Double-check orders before they leave, and include napkins, cutlery and any extras that were requested.

## Make a personal touch

A handwritten "thank you" on the bag, or a free poppadom with a first order, costs little and people remember it.

## Give them a reason to order direct

Include a leaflet or sticker promoting your own website or app with a direct-order discount.

## Loyalty schemes

A simple points system or "every tenth order free" works well. Ordering apps can track this automatically.

## Marketing emails and texts: follow the rules

Under UK GDPR and the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR), you generally need consent to send marketing emails and texts. There is a limited "soft opt-in" for existing customers, but only if:

- you collected their details during a sale
- you're marketing similar products
- you gave them a clear chance to opt out at the time, and in every message.

Keep records of consent and make unsubscribing easy. The Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) website has detailed guidance.

## Listen and respond

Ask for feedback and act on it. A customer whose complaint is handled well often becomes more loyal than one who never had a problem.
