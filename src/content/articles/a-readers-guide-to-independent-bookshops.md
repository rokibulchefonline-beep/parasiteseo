There is a particular pleasure in walking into an independent bookshop without knowing what you'll leave with. From tiny rooms stacked to the ceiling to spacious shops with cafés and event programmes, the UK's independents offer something no algorithm can.

## Ask the booksellers

The staff in an independent shop usually choose what goes on the shelves. Tell them the last three books you loved and ask what they'd suggest next. It's the best recommendation engine there is.

## Look out for events

Many shops host author talks, book clubs and children's story times, often free or for the price of a ticket that doubles as a voucher.

## Order through them

If a shop doesn't have a title in stock, most can order it in, often within a day or two.

## Bookshop crawls

Several towns and cities have enough independents to make a day of it. Plan a route, bring a tote bag, and make time for lunch somewhere local.
