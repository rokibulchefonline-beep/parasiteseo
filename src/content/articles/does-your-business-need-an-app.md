"Should we build an app?" is a question many growing businesses ask. Sometimes the answer is yes, but often a fast, well-designed mobile website delivers most of the benefit.

## When an app makes sense

- **Frequent, repeat use.** Customers who order, book or check in regularly.
- **Loyalty and rewards** that benefit from being in someone's pocket.
- **Push notifications** that customers actually want, such as order updates.
- **Device features** such as the camera, location or offline access.

## When a website is enough

- Most customers find you through search and visit only occasionally.
- Your main goal is enquiries or one-off purchases.
- Your budget is limited. Apps need building for iOS and Android, plus ongoing updates.

## Progressive web apps: a middle ground

A progressive web app (PWA) is a website that behaves more like an app. It can be added to the home screen, work offline and load quickly, without going through app stores.

## Plan for the long term

Apps need ongoing maintenance as iOS and Android change, plus a plan to get people to download them. Budget for both.

## Questions to answer first

1. What will customers do in the app that they can't easily do on the website?
2. How often will they use it?
3. How will you encourage downloads?
4. Who will maintain it?
