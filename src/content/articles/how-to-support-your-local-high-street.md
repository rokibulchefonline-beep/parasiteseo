High streets across the UK have changed enormously over the past two decades. Yet independent shops, cafés and services remain at the heart of many communities. Here are seven things anyone can do to help them thrive.

## 1. Buy one thing a week locally

It doesn't need to be a grand gesture. Picking up bread, a birthday card or a coffee from an independent each week adds up across a town.

## 2. Pay in a way that suits the shop

Card fees can eat into small margins. If a business mentions a preference, it's worth listening.

## 3. Leave a review

Online reviews are a major way new customers find small businesses. A short, honest review costs nothing and can make a real difference.

## 4. Use click and collect from local firms

Many independents now sell online. Ordering from them rather than a national marketplace keeps more money circulating locally.

## 5. Go to the markets

Farmers' markets and makers' markets are often where new businesses test ideas before taking on a shop lease.

## 6. Get involved with your BID or traders' group

Many towns have a Business Improvement District or a traders' association that welcomes volunteers and ideas.

## 7. Tell people

Word of mouth remains the most powerful marketing there is. Recommend the places you love.
