Every neighbourhood has a curry house or takeaway its customers swear by. Public-vote awards give you a way to say thank you.

## Why your vote counts

At the [Asian Restaurant & Takeaway Awards (ARTA)](https://www.artauk.com/), the first stage is driven by the public. Diners nominate and vote for their favourite restaurants and takeaways, and the highest-ranked venues go forward in the competition.

## What you'll be asked to rate

ARTA's Customer Nomination Score looks at three things:

- **Quality of food**
- **Quality of service**
- **Value for money**

Think about your recent visits or orders when you rate each one.

## How to nominate

1. Go to the [ARTA nomination page](https://www.artauk.com/nominate).
2. Find your restaurant or takeaway.
3. Complete the nomination form honestly.

Voting for each year's awards opens in January. Check the ARTA website for the current dates.

## Play fair

Awards only mean something if the votes are genuine. Vote once, rate honestly, and encourage other real customers to do the same. Don't create fake entries or accounts.

## Tell the restaurant

Let the staff know you've nominated them. For a small, family-run business, it can make someone's week.
