A branded app puts your restaurant's logo on a customer's home screen. That's valuable, but only if people actually use it.

## When an app makes sense

- **You have strong repeat custom.** Regulars who order weekly are the most likely to download an app.
- **You want to send push notifications** about offers and new dishes, with the customer's consent.
- **You run a loyalty scheme.** Apps make points and rewards easy to track.

## When a website is enough

If most of your customers order occasionally or are finding you for the first time, a fast mobile ordering website may deliver most of the benefit at lower cost.

## What a good restaurant app includes

- your branding and full menu
- saved addresses and one-tap reordering
- secure payments, including Apple Pay and Google Pay
- order tracking and notifications
- loyalty and offers.

## Getting downloads

An app nobody installs is wasted. Promote it on receipts, bags, menus, your website and social media, and give people a reason to download, such as a discount on their first app order.
