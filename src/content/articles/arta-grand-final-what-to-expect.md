After months of public voting and assessment, the [Asian Restaurant & Takeaway Awards (ARTA)](https://www.artauk.com/awards) reach their climax at the Grand Final.

## When is it?

The 2026 Grand Final takes place in **October 2026**. Check the ARTA website for the confirmed date and venue.

## Who attends

Finalist restaurants and takeaways from across the UK, their teams and families, sponsors, industry figures and guests.

## The awards

- **Regional winners** are announced, recognising the best restaurants and takeaways in each part of the UK.
- The evening culminates in the crowning of the **ARTA Champion of Champions** and the presentation of the ARTA trophy.

## More than a ceremony

For many finalists, the Grand Final is a rare chance to step away from the kitchen, meet peers from across the country and celebrate the industry together.

## Sponsors and partners

The awards are supported by sponsors from across the hospitality and food industry. Businesses interested in reaching Asian restaurant owners can find details on the [ARTA sponsors page](https://www.artauk.com/sponsors).

## Follow the results

Winners are announced on the ARTA website and social channels after the event. It's a great way to find a new favourite restaurant near you.
