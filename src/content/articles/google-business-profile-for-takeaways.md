When someone searches "takeaway near me", Google shows a map with local listings. Those listings come from Google Business Profile, and it's free.

## 1. Claim and verify your profile

Search for your business on Google. If a listing exists, claim it; if not, create one. Google will ask you to verify that you own the business.

## 2. Complete every field

- the exact business name, as shown on your shopfront
- address, phone number and opening hours, including holiday hours
- category, for example "Indian takeaway" or "Bangladeshi restaurant"
- service options: dine-in, takeaway, delivery.

## 3. Add your ordering link

Link to **your own** online ordering page so customers who find you on Google order direct rather than through a marketplace.

## 4. Photos

Upload bright, real photos of your food, your shopfront (so drivers and customers can find you) and your interior. Update them regularly.

## 5. Reviews

Ask happy customers to leave a review, for example with a card in the bag. Reply to every review, especially the negative ones, politely and helpfully. Never buy fake reviews: they break Google's rules and UK consumer law.

## 6. Keep it fresh

Post updates about new dishes, offers and events. Keep your menu current.
