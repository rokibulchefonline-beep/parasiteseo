Welcome to the first edition of The Albion Review, a magazine for readers who want to understand the whole of the United Kingdom, not just the bits that make the national headlines.

## Why another magazine?

Much of the country's media is written from a handful of postcodes. We think the story of Britain is richer than that. It is found in market towns and seaside resorts, in Welsh valleys and Scottish islands, in the terraced streets of the north and the commuter belt of the south.

## What we'll cover

Every week you'll find:

- **News explainers** that set out what's changed and why it matters to you.
- **Business reporting** on the high street, small firms and the cost of living.
- **Culture** from regional theatres, independent bookshops and local festivals.
- **Food & Drink** that celebrates producers, pubs and restaurants beyond the obvious.
- **Travel** ideas for weekends away without leaving the country.

> Good journalism should be useful. If an article doesn't help you understand something or decide something, we haven't done our job.
