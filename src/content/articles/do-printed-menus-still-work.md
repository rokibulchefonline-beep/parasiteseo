It's tempting to think the takeaway menu through the letterbox is a thing of the past. In reality, print remains one of the most reliable ways for a local takeaway to reach its neighbourhood.

## Why print still works

- **It's local.** A leaflet reaches exactly the streets you deliver to.
- **It sticks around.** Menus end up in kitchen drawers and on fridges for months.
- **It reaches everyone**, including customers who don't use apps.

## Make print and digital work together

- Add a **QR code** that goes straight to your online ordering page.
- Print your **website address** prominently, not just a phone number.
- Include a **direct-order offer code** so you can measure responses.

## Design tips

- lead with your best-selling dishes and a strong photo
- keep prices clear and up to date
- show your food hygiene rating if it's a 4 or 5
- include opening hours, delivery area and minimum order.

## Other print that pays

- **Bag stickers and inserts** in every order to encourage direct reordering
- **Loyalty cards**
- **Window posters** promoting your app or website.
