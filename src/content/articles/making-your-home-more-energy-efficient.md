As the nights draw in, small changes around the home can make a noticeable difference to comfort and bills.

## Stop the draughts

Draught excluders, self-adhesive seals around windows and doors, and a cover for the letterbox are all inexpensive.

## Use your heating controls

Setting a timer and turning radiators down in rooms you don't use can help. Thermostatic radiator valves make this easy.

## Curtains and blinds

Close them at dusk to keep the heat in, and open them in the day to let the sun warm your rooms.

## Check what help is available

Energy suppliers, local councils and government schemes sometimes offer grants for insulation and other improvements. The Energy Saving Trust and your council website are good places to start.
