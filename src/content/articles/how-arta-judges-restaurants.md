Awards are only as credible as their judging. Here's how the [Asian Restaurant & Takeaway Awards (ARTA)](https://www.artauk.com/) combine customer opinion with independent measures.

## Stage one: two scores

According to ARTA, restaurants in the first phase are judged on:

**1. Customer Nomination Score**, based on public votes rating:
- quality of food
- quality of service
- value for money.

**2. ARTA Score**, based on independent public data:
- **Tripadvisor reviews**
- **Google reviews**
- **food hygiene ratings**.

Combining the two means a restaurant can't succeed on votes alone. It also needs a consistent reputation with the wider public and a well-run kitchen.

## Moving through the awards

The highest-ranked venues progress through the awards process to the **Grand Final**, where regional winners are announced and one establishment is crowned **ARTA Champion of Champions**.

## What this means for restaurants

The criteria reward the fundamentals: great food, friendly service, fair prices, strong reviews and top hygiene standards. Those are also the things that build a successful restaurant, with or without an award.
