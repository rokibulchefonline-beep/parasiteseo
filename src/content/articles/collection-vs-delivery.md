Delivery is convenient, but if the restaurant is nearby, collecting your order can be the smarter choice.

## Why collection often costs less

- **No delivery fee.** Delivery charges and minimum order values often don't apply to collection.
- **Collection discounts.** Many independents offer a percentage off collection orders because it saves them time and driver costs.
- **No tip for a driver**, although tipping the kitchen is always appreciated.

## It's usually faster

Your food is ready when you arrive, instead of waiting for a driver who may be on another drop. On busy nights, collection can save a good chunk of waiting time.

## It arrives hotter

The shorter the journey, the better the food. Crispy starters, chips and naan in particular are best eaten soon after cooking.

## When delivery makes sense

- You don't have transport or the restaurant isn't within an easy walk or drive.
- The weather is awful.
- You're hosting and can't leave the house.

## How to order for collection

Most restaurant ordering platforms, including [ChefOnline](https://www.chefonline.co.uk/), let you choose collection or delivery at checkout and pick a time slot. Choose a slot that matches how long it takes you to get there, and your order should be ready when you walk in.
