Letting agents usually offer landlords a few service levels. Choosing the right one depends on your time, experience and how close you live to the property.

## Let-only (tenant find)

The agent markets the property, conducts viewings, references tenants and sets up the tenancy. After that, you manage everything yourself.

**Best for:** experienced landlords who live nearby and have time to handle repairs and paperwork.

## Rent collection

As let-only, plus the agent collects rent and chases late payments.

## Full management

The agent handles the whole tenancy:

- rent collection and arrears
- repairs and maintenance, with a network of trusted contractors
- safety certificate renewals (gas, electrical, EPC)
- inspections
- deposit protection and end-of-tenancy deposit returns
- keeping up with legislation such as the Renters' Rights Act.

**Best for:** landlords who live further away, own several properties, or want peace of mind.

## Questions to ask

- What exactly is included, and what costs extra?
- Is the fee a percentage of rent, and is VAT included?
- How are repairs approved and how quickly are they handled?
- Does the agent have client money protection and belong to a redress scheme?
