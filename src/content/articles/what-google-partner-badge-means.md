Many digital agencies display a "Google Partner" badge on their websites. It's a useful signal, but it's worth understanding what it means.

## What it is

Google Partners is Google's programme for advertising agencies that manage Google Ads accounts. To earn the badge, agencies must meet requirements set by Google, which cover areas such as:

- **performance**: maintaining a baseline level of campaign optimisation
- **spend**: managing a minimum level of advertising spend across accounts
- **certifications**: having team members certified in Google Ads.

Google reviews these requirements periodically, so check Google's own Partners pages for the current criteria.

## What it tells you

A Partner agency has demonstrated a level of Google Ads experience and knowledge recognised by Google.

## What it doesn't tell you

- whether the agency understands **your** industry
- how good its communication and reporting are
- how it performs on SEO, social media or web design, which the badge doesn't cover.

## How to check

You can look up agencies in Google's Partners directory to confirm the badge is genuine.

## Questions to ask as well

- Can you show results for businesses like mine?
- Who will manage my account day to day?
- How and how often will you report?
