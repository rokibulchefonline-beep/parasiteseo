Search engine optimisation (SEO) sounds technical, but at its heart it means making it easy for search engines, and people, to understand what your business offers.

## 1. Know what your customers search for

Brainstorm the words customers actually use: "plumber in Leeds", "vegan cake delivery London", "accountant for landlords". Free tools such as Google Search Console show which searches already bring people to your site.

## 2. One page per service

Give each main service its own page with a clear title and heading. A page that tries to cover everything ranks for nothing.

## 3. Write for people first

Answer the questions customers ask: price guides, how the process works, what makes you different. Helpful, original content is what search engines are designed to reward.

## 4. Get the technical basics right

- a fast, mobile-friendly website
- secure HTTPS
- descriptive page titles and meta descriptions
- a sitemap submitted to Google Search Console.

## 5. Local signals

If you serve a local area, claim your Google Business Profile, keep your name, address and phone number consistent everywhere, and collect genuine reviews.

## 6. Earn links, don't buy them

Links from relevant, reputable websites, such as local press, industry bodies and partners, help your site's authority. Buying links breaks Google's guidelines and can harm your rankings.

## 7. Be patient and measure

SEO takes months, not days. Track rankings, traffic and, most importantly, enquiries.
