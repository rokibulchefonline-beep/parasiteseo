Choosing an estate agent is one of the biggest decisions in selling or letting a home. Broadly, you have three options.

## High street chains

**Pros:** recognisable brands, large marketing budgets, multiple branches and a big database of buyers.

**Cons:** staff turnover can be high, and you may deal with a different person at each stage. Targets can mean pressure to accept offers quickly.

## Online and hybrid agents

**Pros:** lower or fixed fees, often paid upfront.

**Cons:** you may have to conduct viewings yourself, and local knowledge can be limited. Some fees are payable even if the property doesn't sell.

## Local independents

**Pros:** deep local knowledge, continuity (you often deal with the same people throughout) and a reputation that depends on word of mouth in the community.

**Cons:** smaller marketing budgets and fewer offices.

## Questions to ask any agent

1. How many similar properties have you sold or let nearby recently?
2. Who will conduct viewings?
3. What's the fee, including VAT, and what's the contract length and notice period?
4. Which redress scheme do you belong to? For letting agents, what client money protection do you have?
5. How will you market my property?
