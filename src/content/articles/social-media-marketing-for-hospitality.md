Food is one of the most shared subjects on social media, which gives restaurants a natural advantage. Here's how to make the most of it.

## Show the food, and the people

- short videos of dishes being cooked, plated or served
- behind-the-scenes clips of the kitchen and team
- regular customers (with their permission)
- the story behind signature dishes.

## Consistency beats perfection

Posting regularly with good-enough phone footage usually beats occasional polished posts. Plan a simple weekly routine.

## Make it easy to order or book

Put your ordering link, booking link, address and opening hours in your bio. Every post should make it obvious how to buy.

## Use local targeting

Tag your location, use local hashtags, and run small paid campaigns targeted to people within your delivery or travel radius.

## Engage

Reply to comments and messages quickly. Share (with permission) posts from customers who tag you.

## Promote offers carefully

Advertising rules apply on social media too. Offers must be clear and truthful, and influencer posts must be labelled as ads under ASA and CMA guidance.

## Measure

Track which posts lead to website visits, orders or bookings, not just likes.
