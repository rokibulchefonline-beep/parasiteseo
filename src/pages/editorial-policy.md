## Accuracy

We check facts against primary sources wherever possible and name our sources unless there is a good reason not to.

## Corrections

If we get something wrong we correct it promptly and note the change at the end of the article, including the date. To report an error, email us using the address on our [contact page](/contact/).

## Independence and commercial content

Our journalism is editorially independent. Paid partnerships are labelled **"Advertisement feature"** at the top of the article, and links that earn us a commission are disclosed, in line with the CAP Code and Advertising Standards Authority guidance.

## Complaints

We aim to acknowledge complaints within five working days.
