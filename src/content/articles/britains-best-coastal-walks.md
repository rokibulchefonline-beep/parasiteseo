The UK has a remarkably long and varied coastline, and much of it is open to walkers. The King Charles III England Coast Path, the Wales Coast Path and Scotland's many coastal routes make it easier than ever.

## Check the tides

Some beaches and headlands are cut off at high tide. Look up tide times before you set out.

## Choose the right distance

A linear walk with a bus or train back is often more rewarding than a circular route. Plan the return leg first.

## Pack for changeable weather

Layers, waterproofs and sturdy footwear, even in summer.

## Respect the countryside

Follow the Countryside Code: leave gates as you find them, keep dogs under control near livestock and take your litter home.
