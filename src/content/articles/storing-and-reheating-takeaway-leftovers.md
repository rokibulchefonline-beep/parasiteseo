Leftover curry can taste even better the next day, but only if it's stored and reheated safely. Here's what the Food Standards Agency (FSA) recommends.

## Cool it quickly

Don't leave leftovers out all evening. Cool them as quickly as possible and put them in the fridge. Splitting large portions into shallow containers helps them cool faster.

## Take special care with rice

Cooked rice can contain spores of *Bacillus cereus*, which can survive cooking and cause food poisoning if rice is left warm. The FSA advises:

- cool rice as quickly as possible, ideally within an hour
- keep it in the fridge for no more than a day before reheating
- reheat it only once, until it is steaming hot all the way through.

## Storing other leftovers

The FSA advises eating leftovers within two days, or within 24 hours if they contain cooked rice. Keep your fridge at 5°C or below.

## Freezing

Most curries freeze well. Freeze them once they've cooled, label them with the date, and defrost them thoroughly in the fridge before reheating.

## Reheating

Reheat until the food is steaming hot throughout, stirring halfway if you're using a microwave. Don't reheat leftovers more than once.

## Order less, waste less

Ordering to share, with more sides and fewer duplicate mains, is the easiest way to avoid leftovers. When ordering from local restaurants on [ChefOnline](https://www.chefonline.co.uk/), you can ask the restaurant about portion sizes when you order.
