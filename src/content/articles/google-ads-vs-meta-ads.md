Both Google and Meta (Facebook and Instagram) offer powerful advertising tools, but they work in very different ways.

## Google Ads: capturing demand

Search ads appear when someone is actively looking for something, for example "emergency locksmith Croydon". You're reaching people **with intent**.

**Best for:** services people search for, urgent needs, and products with clear search demand.

## Meta Ads: creating demand

Facebook and Instagram ads reach people based on their interests, demographics and behaviour, often before they know they need you.

**Best for:** visual products, events, new brands, restaurants and offers, and building awareness in a local area.

## Budgets and costs

Both platforms work on an auction, so costs vary by industry and competition. Start with a modest test budget, measure results and scale up what works.

## Measure what matters

Clicks and likes aren't the goal: enquiries, bookings and sales are. Set up conversion tracking before you spend.

## Using both together

Many businesses use Meta to build awareness and retarget visitors, and Google Search to capture people who are ready to buy.
