Buying your first home in London is a big step. Here's the process in plain English.

## 1. Work out your budget

Your budget depends on your deposit and how much a lender will offer. Use mortgage calculators, but speak to a mortgage adviser or broker for an accurate picture. Remember to budget for:

- solicitor's fees and searches
- a survey
- Stamp Duty Land Tax, if it applies (first-time buyers may qualify for relief, so check the current thresholds on GOV.UK)
- moving costs and furniture.

## 2. Build your deposit

A larger deposit usually means better mortgage rates. A Lifetime ISA can add a government bonus for eligible first-time buyers, but check the rules and property price limit carefully.

## 3. Get an agreement in principle

An agreement in principle from a lender shows agents and sellers that you're a serious buyer.

## 4. Understand leasehold

Many London flats are leasehold. Check the lease length, service charges, ground rent and any building safety issues before you commit.

## 5. Make an offer

Your agent will pass your offer to the seller. Be ready to show your agreement in principle and proof of deposit.

## 6. Conveyancing and survey

Your solicitor carries out searches and checks the legal title. A survey checks the building's condition.

## 7. Exchange and completion

Once you exchange contracts, you're legally committed. On completion day, you get the keys.
